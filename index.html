Why SoundSee

Cinema, without the barriers.

Audio Description for audiences who are blind or have low vision. Closed captions for audiences who are deaf or hard of hearing. One app, one film, any screen.

Audio Description

A narrator describes the action, expressions and scenes between the dialogue — heard only by you, through your own headphones.

Closed Captions

Time-coded captions for dialogue and key sounds, on your own phone — so the screen stays as the filmmaker intended.

Any screen, any show

Theatre, TV, laptop or an OTT platform. If the film is playing, SoundSee can play along with it.

Stay in sync

Joined late or drifted off? Resync with a tap so the narration lands on the right scene again.

How it works

Three taps to the full experience.

1

Pick a film

Choose a film in the app and download its audio track in your preferred language.

2

Start the film anywhere

Press play on any screen — a TV, a laptop, an OTT platform, a theatre.

3

Tap Play in SoundSee

Start at the same moment and hear the description or the dialogue through your headphones, in step with the film.

Built for the CBFC accessibility requirement

Audio Description and captions are part of the CBFC certification process for feature films in India, and delivery through an app over headphones is allowed. Deka Xoinyaxi hosted its AD and CC on SoundSee and received its CBFC certificate.
