Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 April 2026 · Last updated: 11 August 2026

This privacy policy explains how SoundSee ("the app") — published by SoniqRyder — collects, uses, and safeguards your information. Please read it carefully. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

If any section is unclear, reach us at contact@soundsee.in.

1. Who we are

SoundSee is a mobile application that delivers audio description (AD) and synced closed captions (CC) for independent films in Indian languages. The AD audio plays privately through your headphones while the film plays on any nearby screen — theatre, TV, laptop, or streaming service.

Publisher: SoniqRyder
Contact: contact@soundsee.in

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to operate the service.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Mobile number. Required to create an account. You sign in with a one-time password (OTP) we send via SMS — we do not store any password.
  • First name, last name, preferred audio language. Optional, shown on your profile.
  • Profile photo (avatar). Optional, uploaded by you.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Authentication tokens. After a successful OTP sign-in, your device stores a short-lived access token and a refresh token so you don't have to sign in every time. These are stored in the device's secure credential storage (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences / iOS Keychain) and are never written to plain files.
  • Download history. When you download an audio track for offline playback, we record the track ID, a timestamp, and a 24-hour expiry so the app knows when to prompt for a re-download.
  • Device-local preferences. Your "require headphones for AD" toggle is saved locally on your device only (SharedPreferences / UserDefaults). This preference is not sent to our servers.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs. Generated by the operating system (Google Play Store / Apple App Store) when the app crashes. These may include device model, OS version, and a stack trace. They are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photo library, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not track your listening habits for advertising.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your session active.
  • Display your profile and save your preferences across devices.
  • Serve the correct audio and caption files for the films you download.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not use your data for profiling, advertising, or analytics beyond the crash reports generated automatically by the app stores.

5. Data retention

  • Mobile number and profile information: kept as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of an account-deletion request.
  • Authentication tokens: kept until they expire (access tokens: 1 hour; refresh tokens: up to 30 days of inactivity) or until you sign out.
  • Download metadata: expires automatically 24 hours after each download.
  • Audio and caption files: stored indefinitely in our content library, not tied to any individual user.

6. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it by editing your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated personal data by emailing contact@soundsee.in with the subject "Delete my account".
  • Withdraw consent by signing out and uninstalling the app.

We will respond to any such request within 30 days.

7. Security

We protect your information through standard industry practices:

  • HTTPS/TLS for every network request between the app and our backend.
  • Auth tokens stored in OS-provided secure credential storage on your device.
  • Supabase-managed database access with short-lived signed URLs for audio and caption downloads.
  • Role-based access controls on our infrastructure so only authorised operators can view user data.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; if we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

8. Children's privacy

SoundSee is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact contact@soundsee.in and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our backend and storage providers may process data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we rely on the provider's standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above and — for material changes — notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The latest version will always be available on this page.
